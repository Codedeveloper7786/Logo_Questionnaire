Introduction

Welcome to Mirza Logo Designing ("we," "us," or "our"). We are a creative studio specialising in professional logo design and brand identity services for businesses and individuals worldwide.

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, contact us for services, or engage with our design process. Please read this policy carefully. If you disagree with its terms, please discontinue use of our services.

By using our services or website, you consent to the data practices described in this Privacy Policy. This policy applies to all clients, visitors, and individuals who interact with Mirza Logo Designing.

02

Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to deliver our logo design and branding services effectively. The types of information we may gather include:

👤
Personal Identifiers
Name, email address, phone number, and billing information provided when engaging our services.
💼
Business Information
Company name, industry, brand preferences, and creative briefs you share with our team.
💳
Payment Details
Transaction data processed securely via third-party processors. We do not store card details.
🌐
Usage Data
IP address, browser type, pages visited, and time spent on our site via cookies and analytics.

We collect this information when you fill out a contact or order form, correspond with us by email, make a payment, or browse our portfolio website.

03

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used exclusively to serve you better and operate our business effectively. We use your data to:

  • Deliver and manage your logo design and branding projects from brief to final delivery.
  • Communicate with you throughout the design process, including revisions and approvals.
  • Process payments and issue invoices for services rendered.
  • Respond to your enquiries, feedback, and support requests promptly.
  • Send you important service-related notifications and project status updates.
  • Improve our website, portfolio, and overall service quality based on aggregated usage patterns.
  • Send occasional marketing emails about our services — only with your explicit consent, and you may opt out at any time.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations and protect our legal rights.

We process your data on the legal bases of contractual necessity, legitimate business interest, and — where required by law — your explicit consent.

04

Sharing of Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to any third party. We value your privacy and only share your data in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: Trusted partners who assist in delivering our services — such as payment processors, email platforms, and cloud storage providers — are bound by strict confidentiality agreements and may only use your data for the purposes we specify.
  • Legal Compliance: When required by law, court order, or a legitimate government request, we may disclose information to comply with our legal obligations.
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your data may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will provide notice before your data is transferred and becomes subject to a different privacy policy.
  • With Your Consent: We may share your project information — such as for our portfolio or case studies — only upon receiving your prior written consent.

We never display your completed logo or brand assets in our portfolio without first receiving explicit written permission from you.

05

Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes outlined in this policy. Specifically:

  • Project files and correspondence are retained for up to 3 years after project completion to support revision requests or resolve any disputes.
  • Financial records, including invoices and payment data, are kept for 7 years to meet tax and accounting obligations.
  • Marketing-related data is retained until you withdraw consent or unsubscribe from our communications.
  • Website usage data collected via cookies is typically retained for 12 to 24 months.

Upon the expiry of these periods, or upon a valid deletion request from you, we securely delete or anonymise your data in accordance with applicable data protection law.

06

Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correction: Ask us to correct any inaccurate or incomplete information we hold.
  • Deletion: Request that we delete your personal data, subject to any legal retention obligations.
  • Opt-Out: Unsubscribe from marketing communications at any time via the unsubscribe link included in every email.
  • Data Portability: Request a copy of your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Objection: Object to the processing of your personal data for direct marketing or legitimate interest purposes.
  • Withdraw Consent: Where we process your data on the basis of consent, you may withdraw that consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details provided in Section 10 below. We will respond to all valid requests within 30 days.

07

Security

We take the security of your personal information seriously and implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect it against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

  • All data transmissions between your browser and our website are encrypted using industry-standard SSL/TLS protocols.
  • Access to client files and personal data is restricted to authorised personnel only, on a need-to-know basis.
  • We use secure, access-controlled cloud storage services with regular security reviews.
  • Payment information is handled exclusively by PCI-DSS compliant third-party processors and is never stored on our servers.

While we apply every reasonable precaution, no method of transmission over the internet is entirely secure. In the unlikely event of a data breach that may affect your rights or freedoms, we will notify you in accordance with applicable law without undue delay.

08

Children's Privacy

Mirza Logo Designing does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from individuals under the age of 18. Our services are intended for business owners, entrepreneurs, and adults seeking professional creative services.

If you believe we have inadvertently collected personal information from a minor, please contact us immediately at the details below and we will promptly delete such data from our records.

We encourage you to review this policy periodically to stay informed about how we protect your information. Your continued use of our services after any changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
